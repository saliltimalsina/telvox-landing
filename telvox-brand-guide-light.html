06 — Do's & Don'ts

Do

  • Use the dark logo on light or off-white backgrounds
  • Maintain lowercase "telvox" wordmark
  • Use the orb as the signature icon
  • Apply Signal Purple as the accent on light UI
  • Keep generous whitespace around logo
  • Use DM Sans for all brand typography

Don't

  • Capitalize or alter the wordmark
  • Place the white logo on light backgrounds
  • Set body copy in neon cyan on white — it fails contrast
  • Stretch or distort proportions
  • Use off-brand colors or fonts
  • Apply drop shadows to the wordmark
07 — Brand Voice & Motion

Brand Voice

  • Confident but not arrogant
  • Technical clarity, human warmth
  • Concise — every word earns its place
  • Future-forward, not jargon-heavy
  • Empowering, never condescending

Motion Principles

  • Orb pulses: slow, breathing rhythm
  • Gradients shift softly — never flash
  • Transitions: ease-in-out, 300–500ms
  • Particle trails follow cyan → magenta
  • Hover states: gentle glow uplift
